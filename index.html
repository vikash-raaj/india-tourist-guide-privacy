India Travel Guide

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

India Travel Guide ("we", "our", or "the app") is an independent travel reference app that helps users explore tourist destinations across India. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of India or any tourism authority. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and your choices.

1. Information We Collect

  • Favourites — destinations you save are stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.
  • Advertising data (collected by Google AdMob) — the app shows banner ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and ad interaction data (e.g. impressions and clicks) to serve, measure and, where you have consented, personalise ads.

We do not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment information or precise location. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

2. How Information Is Used

  • To remember the destinations you have saved as favourites.
  • To display ads that support keeping the app free (via Google AdMob).
  • To measure ad performance and prevent ad fraud (via Google AdMob).

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google AdMob — advertising. See How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps (external) — tapping "Get Directions" opens Google Maps in your browser or the Maps app. The app itself does not embed maps or access your location.
  • Image hosting — destination photos are loaded from Unsplash; your device's IP address is visible to their servers when images load.

Data sent to Google AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

4. Consent and Your Ad Choices

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the app asks for your consent before showing personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time using Ad privacy at the bottom of the home screen.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

5. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

We operate no servers and do not receive or store any of your personal information. Favourites remain on your device until you remove them in the app, clear the app's data (Settings → Apps → India Travel Guide → Storage → Clear Data), or uninstall the app. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies; you can manage it at My Ad Center.

6. Permissions

  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — to load destination images and ads.
  • com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID — used by Google AdMob to access the advertising ID.

7. Children's Privacy

India Travel Guide is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
